Bike Rental Guidelines & Company Policies

Please read and agree to all terms and policies before continuing:

1. General Guidelines
  • The renter must present a valid driver’s license before bike handover.
  • The renter is fully responsible for any damage, loss, or fines incurred during the rental period.
  • Helmets are mandatory at all times while riding.
  • Bikes must be returned with the same fuel level as received.
  • Late returns will incur extra charges based on hourly rent.
  • In case of accident or damage, the renter must inform us immediately.
  • No refunds for early returns or cancellations after pickup.
2. Customer Verification & Documentation
  • Customer must provide an original driving license (two-wheeler license mandatory).
  • A copy of Aadhaar / Passport / other ID proof must be collected.
  • Customer’s mobile number and emergency contact must be verified.
  • A rental agreement form must be filled and signed before handing over the keys.
3. Rental Policy & Payment
  • Minimum rental duration: 4 hours.
  • Full payment must be collected in advance.
  • Security deposit: ₹1000 (varies by vehicle), refundable within 20 days.
  • Fuel is not included unless specified.
  • Late return charges apply after a 15-minute grace period.
  • No partial refund for early returns.

4. Damage, Theft & Accident Policy
  • The customer is fully responsible for any damage, loss, or theft during the rental period.
  • In case of an accident:
    • Inform Rosa Waves immediately.
    • A police complaint must be filed.
    • Repair costs will be charged as per service invoice.
  • Insurance claim procedures (if applicable) must be followed strictly.
